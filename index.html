About us

A campus built for how children actually learn

We keep class sizes small, teachers close at hand, and the timetable balanced between books, labs, playing fields and the arts — because none of those are extras.

  • 01
    Small classrooms. No more than 30 students per section, from Nursery through Class XII.
  • 02
    CBSE curriculum. Delivered with hands-on labs, project work and regular assessment, not just textbooks.
  • 03
    Whole-child focus. Sport, music, art and clubs are timetabled, not optional add-ons.
Academics

Three stages, one continuous journey

Each stage has its own pace and space, so children are never rushed ahead or held back.

Pre-Primary

Nursery – UKG

Play-based learning that builds language, motor skills and confidence through stories, art and guided play.

Primary

Class I – V

Core subjects paired with science labs, library periods and the first steps into a second language.

High School

Class VI – XII

Streamed electives, exam preparation and career counselling, alongside sport, debate and student council.

Everything a school day needs, on one campus

Science labs
